These terms apply to Prompt Batch Pro, the paid licence for the Prompt Batch Chrome extension. By buying Pro or activating a licence key you agree to them.

1. What you get

A personal, non-transferable licence for one person to use Prompt Batch Pro on up to 2 computers or Chrome accounts that belong to that person. Depending on how you pay, Pro is sold as:

  • Monthly subscription (card or PayPal): stays active while the subscription is paid; if you cancel, Pro keeps working until the end of the paid month.
  • Yearly licence (InstaPay or Vodafone Cash): Pro for 12 months from the day it is approved. It does not renew by itself; to keep Pro after that, you buy it again.
  • Lifetime licence: paid once. "Lifetime" means for as long as Prompt Batch is offered and updated, not forever.

Updates are included for as long as the extension is offered.

2. No sharing

  • Do not share, give, sell, rent or publish your licence key, and do not let other people use your Pro activation.

Your licence key stays yours. If you remove the extension or change computer, install it again and activate the same key, on up to 2 computers at a time. If both are taken, remove Pro from an old computer (⚙ → Pro & licence → Remove from this computer) or ask us through the support form.
